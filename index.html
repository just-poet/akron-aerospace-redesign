01 The mission

Across the water.
Direct to the island.

When a ferry is the only connection, an urgent shipment moves on its schedule. This concept shows how a small cargo drone could connect two islands on demand.

The route in three steps

  1. 01

    Dispatch from the origin island

    The sender secures a priority parcel or medical case and confirms who will receive it.

  2. 02

    Cross the water directly

    A dedicated air route could help where ferry schedules or road detours make urgent deliveries unpredictable.

  3. 03

    Confirm the handoff

    The receiving team checks the shipment. A pilot would measure time to receipt and, for sensitive supplies, condition on arrival.

05 Our impact

What a better route
could change.

We want to make urgent delivery more dependable for people and businesses. Each proposed route should earn its place by improving on the delivery option already in use.

01 / Access

Bring essential supplies closer

A direct route could give remote clinics and island communities another way to receive urgent supplies when roads or ferries add hours.

Measure Request-to-receipt time and delivery completion.

02 / Reliability

Keep services running

A missing spare part can stop a repair or shut down a site. A targeted flight could get the part to a field team sooner and reduce downtime.

Measure Delivery time, downtime and cost per shipment.

03 / Stewardship

Use energy where it counts

A small electric aircraft could replace a dedicated vehicle trip on some routes. The benefit depends on the payload, charging source and ground trips it actually replaces.

Measure Energy, emissions and local noise against the existing route.

Independent research helps frame what we would measure. Rwanda blood delivery study · Drone delivery life-cycle study

06 Why Akron

Prove one route.
Then grow.

Akron brings a medical-first design mindset to both missions. A shipment needs to arrive in the right condition, with a clear record of who sent and received it. The goal is to make that handoff repeatable before expanding to more routes.

  1. 01 / Current stage

    Develop the prototype

    Refine the aircraft and payload concepts around the needs of real senders and receivers.

  2. 02 / Planned

    Validate the handoff

    Test payload security, condition on arrival and recovery procedures in controlled trials.

  3. 03 / Proposed

    Pilot a defined route

    Work with a partner on a specific origin and destination, then compare reliability, cost and delivery time with their existing option.
